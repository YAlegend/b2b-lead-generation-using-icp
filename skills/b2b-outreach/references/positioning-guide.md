# Positioning Guide — Phase 1

## The Formula

> "[Company] helps [specific buyer] [achieve outcome] without [common obstacle]."

Short version:
> "[What you do] for [who] — [the outcome they care about]."

## Rules

- One sentence. No conjunctions splitting it into two ideas.
- Name the buyer explicitly — not "businesses" or "companies"
- Name the outcome the buyer cares about, not the feature or service you deliver
- If there is a minimum deal size, the language should imply it
- Never use: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "innovative", "solutions", "synergy"

## Examples Across Industries

- SaaS: "We help finance teams close their books 3x faster — without replacing their ERP."
- Recruitment: "We place senior engineers at Series B startups — in under 3 weeks."
- Legal: "We handle IP disputes for tech founders — so they can stay focused on building."
- Consulting: "We run digital transformation programmes for NHS trusts — on time, in budget."
- Design agency: "We build brand identities for consumer startups preparing for launch."

These are illustrations only. The actual statement must come from the company being analysed.

## Vertical Variants

Write one variant per ICP vertical. Each variant:
- Keeps the same core promise
- Swaps the buyer descriptor and outcome to match that vertical's language
- References a vertical-specific result or constraint where relevant

## LinkedIn About (First 2 Lines)

These are the only lines visible before "see more" on mobile.
Two sentences that must together:
1. State who you help
2. State what you do for them
3. Create enough curiosity to click "see more"

## Tagline

8 words or fewer. Works as a homepage hero headline.
Test: could it go on a business card?
