# Labtronics Design Ltd — Product Description

Labtronics Design Ltd is a UK-based electronics product design consultancy that takes hardware
products from concept through to production-ready design. We handle the full engineering stack —
schematic design, PCB layout, embedded firmware, DFM review, and pre-certification support —
so founders and product teams do not need to hire in-house hardware engineers.

Our clients are companies that have a product idea, a prototype, or a half-built device and need
an expert team to bring it to manufacturable, certifiable hardware. We work on medical wearables,
connected health devices, industrial IoT sensors, payment terminals, and consumer electronics.

The minimum engagement size is £15,000. We are not a fit for hobbyists, academia, or companies
that have not secured funding or a clear commercial route to market.

## What we deliver

- Full hardware design: schematics, PCB layout, bill of materials, DFM-ready Gerbers
- Embedded firmware: bare-metal and RTOS (FreeRTOS), BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi, USB, I2C, SPI
- Regulatory preparation: IEC 60601, CE marking, pre-EMC, design for IEC 62368
- Prototype to production handover: design for manufacture, factory liaison, NPI support

## Representative work

- U-RHYTHM (Dynamic Therapeutics): ambulatory hormone profiling wearable — full hardware
  and firmware design for a patch-based biosensor platform
- Kynekt: LoRa mesh tool-tracking device — power-optimised firmware and RF design
- ConnectomX: high-voltage microtomy electronics — custom analogue front-end and safety
  interlocking system
- Redrock/MSoft360: sports impact mouthguard — miniaturised IMU platform and BLE streaming

## Why clients choose us

- We understand regulatory constraints for medical and industrial hardware — they are built into
  the design, not bolted on at the end
- We are a small, senior team — there is no layer of juniors between the client and the engineers
  doing the work
- We carry projects from blank sheet to production-ready, so the client deals with one firm, not
  a chain of handoffs between PCB designers, firmware houses, and DFM consultants
