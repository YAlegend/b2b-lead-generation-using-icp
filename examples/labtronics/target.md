# Labtronics Design — Target Market Definition

## Who we are looking for

We are looking for decision-makers at early-stage companies that are building a physical hardware
product and do not have a sufficient in-house electronics engineering team to take it to market.

The ideal contact is a **non-hardware technical founder**, a **CEO**, a **CTO with a software
background**, or a **Head of Product** at a company where the core team's expertise is in software,
data, biology, or business — but the product they are building requires custom electronics.

These people feel the problem acutely: they know what they want to ship, they cannot hire a
senior hardware engineer fast enough or at all, and they are either stuck at prototype stage or
heading toward it.

---

## Vertical 1 — MedTech and Connected Health

**Company type:** Early-stage medical device startup, connected health startup, digital therapeutics
company, or health-monitoring wearable company.

**Stage:** Pre-seed to Series B, typically 2–30 employees. Has received SBIR/UKRI/Innovate UK
grant funding, angel investment, or early VC. Has a working concept or early prototype but
needs to reach a manufacturable, regulatorily-sound device design.

**Job titles to target:** CEO, CTO, Co-Founder, VP of Engineering (software background), Head of
Product, Chief Medical Officer who is also running the technical build.

**Buying signal keywords in their profile or company description:** "medical wearable", "remote
patient monitoring", "digital biomarker", "biosensor", "FDA clearance", "510(k)", "IEC 60601",
"CE marking", "connected health", "health-tech", "clinical-grade", "wearable diagnostics",
"point-of-care", "implantable", "ambulatory monitoring".

**What they are NOT:** NHS procurement teams, hospital groups, pharma R&D, academic research labs.
We want commercial startups building a product they intend to sell.

---

## Vertical 2 — IoT Hardware Startups

**Company type:** Startup or scaleup building a physical IoT device for consumer, industrial, or
commercial use. The device communicates wirelessly (BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi, NB-IoT, Zigbee) and is
intended for volume production.

**Stage:** Seed to Series B. Has raised money and is on a timeline to ship. May have a working
proof-of-concept but needs production-grade PCB design, firmware, and DFM.

**Job titles to target:** CEO, CTO, Co-Founder, VP of Product, Director of Engineering, Head of
Hardware (if the role exists but is junior or software-heavy).

**Buying signal keywords:** "IoT platform", "smart device", "connected device", "wireless sensor",
"hardware startup", "asset tracking", "remote monitoring", "edge device", "smart building",
"predictive maintenance", "condition monitoring", "LPWAN", "LoRaWAN", "BLE beacon",
"embedded systems", "firmware development".

**What they are NOT:** pure software IoT platform companies with no physical device, OEM resellers
buying off-the-shelf modules and not designing hardware.

---

## Vertical 3 — Retail and Payment Hardware

**Company type:** Retail-tech startup, payment hardware company, or a software company adding a
hardware touchpoint to an existing product. Building POS terminals, smart kiosks, RFID/NFC
readers, smart shelf hardware, or bespoke retail automation devices.

**Stage:** Seed to Series B, or an SME that has a proven software product and is now adding
hardware. May already have revenue from a software business.

**Job titles to target:** CTO, Head of Engineering, Director of Product, Co-Founder, VP of
Hardware, VP of Technology.

**Buying signal keywords:** "payment terminal", "POS hardware", "retail kiosk", "smart shelf",
"RFID", "NFC payment", "contactless", "self-checkout", "loss prevention hardware",
"retail automation", "inventory hardware".

---

## Vertical 4 — Industrial IoT and Energy Monitoring

**Company type:** Industrial tech startup or SME building sensor hardware for predictive maintenance,
energy monitoring, environmental sensing, or condition monitoring in manufacturing,
utilities, or infrastructure.

**Stage:** Seed to Series B, or a well-funded SME. May have won a pilot contract with a large
industrial customer and needs to productionise.

**Job titles to target:** CTO, Chief Engineer, Director of Engineering, VP of Product, Co-Founder.

**Buying signal keywords:** "predictive maintenance", "condition monitoring", "vibration sensor",
"energy monitoring", "smart meter", "industrial IoT", "IIoT", "asset monitoring",
"infrastructure sensor", "utility monitoring", "emissions monitoring", "environmental sensor".

---

## Disqualifying signals (do not target)

- Academic or research institutions (universities, national labs)
- Large enterprise hardware manufacturers with in-house engineering (>500 employees)
- Companies explicitly advertising for an in-house Head of Hardware (they are building a team,
  not outsourcing)
- Consumer electronics hobbyist platforms (Arduino resellers, Kickstarter gadgets under £50k)
- Companies with no funding and no stated commercial route to market

---

## Geography

Primary: United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, UAE/MENA.
Secondary: Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark (English-language comms expected).

---

## What a good lead looks like (summary)

A funded, early-stage company building a physical device in medtech, IoT, retail-tech, or
industrial monitoring, where the decision-maker has a software or business background and
needs an expert electronics design partner to take them from prototype to production, with a
project budget above £15,000.
