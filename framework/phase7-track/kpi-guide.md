## The Funnel Math

```
200 prospects contacted
  × 5% reply rate         = 10 replies
  × 60% book a call       = 6 discovery calls
  × 50% request proposal  = 3 proposals
  × 40% close rate        = 1–2 closed deals
```

Adjust multipliers based on:
- Warm vs cold list (warm: 2–3× better reply rates)
- Personalisation level (high personalisation: 2× reply rate)
- Deal size (higher deal size = longer cycle, lower close rate)

## Daily Rhythm (Weeks 2–4)

- 30 min: Send / follow up on emails (25–30 per day)
- 15 min: LinkedIn — engage with 5 prospect posts
- 15 min: Update HubSpot pipeline
- 45 min (call days): Discovery calls + same-day follow-up email

## Red Flags to Watch

- Open rate < 20%: Subject lines or sender domain is the problem
- Reply rate < 2%: Email content or ICP targeting is off
- Call → proposal < 30%: Discovery script needs work
- No replies after 150 sends: ICP may be wrong — revisit target.md
