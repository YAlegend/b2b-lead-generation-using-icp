## Mailbox Warmup

Before sending cold email from a new Gmail:
- Send 10 emails/day for days 1–3
- Send 20 emails/day for days 4–7
- Send 30 emails/day from day 8 onwards
- Never send more than 50/day from a free Gmail to cold contacts
- OpenOutreach handles this pacing automatically
