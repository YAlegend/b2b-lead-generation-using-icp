# Discovery Call Guide — Phase 5

## 30-Minute Structure

- 0:00–2:00  — Icebreaker + set the agenda
- 2:00–15:00 — Discovery questions (you listen 80% of the time)
- 15:00–22:00 — Position your company (tailored to what they said)
- 22:00–28:00 — Handle questions / objections
- 28:00–30:00 — Next steps + close

## BANT Questions to Always Cover

**Budget**
- "Do you have a rough budget allocated for this project?"
- "Are you looking for a fixed-price engagement or time-and-materials?"

**Authority**
- "Who else is involved in the decision to bring in an external partner?"
- "Is there a procurement or legal process we'd need to go through?"

**Need**
- "What's the biggest blocker between where you are now and your launch date?"
- "Have you worked with external design partners before? What worked / didn't?"

**Timeline**
- "What's your target date for [next milestone]?"
- "Is there a funding round, customer deadline, or event driving the timeline?"

## The Positioning Window (minutes 15–22)

Only after you've heard their situation:
1. Reflect back what you heard ("So the main challenge is X")
2. Connect it to your capability ("That's exactly where we tend to add the most value")
3. Give one specific proof point ("We did something similar for [client]")
4. Invite their reaction ("Does that match what you're looking for?")

## Handling "We're Not Ready Yet"

Response A (timeline probe):
"Totally understand — when do you think the timing would be right?
Most of our projects take [X weeks] to kick off from first call, so
if you're targeting [their milestone], we'd ideally start conversations [X weeks] before."

Response B (low-stakes offer):
"Would it be useful to have a 20-minute technical scoping call before you're ready?
No commitment — it often helps teams like yours crystallise what they actually need."

## Closing the Call

"Based on what you've shared, I think there's a strong fit. The next step I'd suggest
is [a scoping doc / a proposal / a follow-up call with your CTO].
I can have that to you by [specific date]. Does that work?"
